# Archetypes

Each character fits loosely into one archetype, and it's common to fit more
than one. An archetype describes a role in a fight, not a rule the engine
enforces.

## Warrior

- Strong offense and strong defense.
- Should be able to win a one-on-one against any other class.
- Plays to team synergy less than the others.
- Gets better later in a fight, once a few characters have been knocked out.
  This comes from their stats winning one-on-ones, not from an archetype
  mechanic; individual Warriors may still have moves that lean into it.

## Rogue

- Kills or disables the opponent that is the biggest threat.
- Tends to be fast, with high attack and low defense. Fast is the Rogues'
  niche: most of them are Fast.

## Tank

- The damage sponge.
- Provides crowd control through taunts or status effects.
- Doesn't deal much damage.

## Mage

- Damage and effects that hit an area.
- Strong early in a fight. The area damage falls off later only because there
  are fewer targets left to hit; it is not weaker per target.

## Support

- Helps their own team with buffs, heals and shields rather than attacking.
