# Draft

Teams are built by a draft before the battle.

- Every character in the roster ([characters.md](characters.md)) is available.
- Once a character is picked, it is gone: no one else can pick it. So a
  battle never has the same character twice.
- Each team drafts 4.

## Home and away

Before the draft, a coin flip decides whether the player is home or away, and
the game says which. (Letting the player choose instead is an option for
later.)

## Pick order

The away team picks first, then the picks snake two at a time:

| Pick | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|------|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Team | Away | Home | Home | Away | Away | Home | Home | Away |

Away gets the first pick to make up for home's priority in the Fast phase
([speed.md](speed.md)), which is very strong.

## Slots

Each team's characters take slots in the order they were drafted: its first
pick is slot 0. Slot order decides who goes first within a tier
([speed.md](speed.md)).

## The foe's picks

For now the foe drafts at random, with one rule: it doesn't pick a character
whose archetype ([archetypes.md](archetypes.md)) its team already has, unless
every character left would repeat one. With five archetypes and four picks,
that rarely happens.
